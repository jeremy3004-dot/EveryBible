Thanks for testing Every Bible 1.0.9. This build fixes a lot since the last one. Please try these:

- Share a verse as an image: tap a verse, choose Image, pick a background and share it. The share sheet should open every time, and the reference should be easy to read. Try a Bible in another script too, such as Hindi or Arabic.
- Listen for a few minutes, then open More > Reading activity and your profile. Your listening time should show there.
- Audio: pause and play with your headphones' button. Pause a chapter, take a phone call, and check it stays paused after the call. Set a sleep timer and let it run out: the next chapter should not start. With auto-advance on, chapters without audio should be skipped.
- Discreet mode: turn it on, then change your profile photo or record voice feedback. The app should not lock while the photo picker or microphone prompt is open. Leave the app from the reader, come back and unlock: you should be where you were.
- Daily reminder: turn it on from another phone signed in to the same account, then open Settings on this phone. You should see a one-tap button to allow notifications.
- Also check: plan days and the reading calendar after midnight (the calendar should show full weeks), a Bible you switch to while offline is still chosen after you reopen the app, and a search with no results says "No verses match your search."

Found a problem? Take a screenshot to send TestFlight feedback, or email hello@everybible.app.
