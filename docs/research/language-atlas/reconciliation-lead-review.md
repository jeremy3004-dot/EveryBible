## Evidence standard

A saved crosswalk is not necessarily provider-confirmed. Inspection found that the 5,237 populated `Official Glottolog Exact Match Glottocode` cells in the saved workbook were generated by name/parent matching: 5,120 colon-suffix/ancestor-ISO joins and 117 normalized-name joins. These are candidate evidence, not independent identifier proof. No runtime fuzzy/name merge is permitted.

A reviewed decision identifies exact canonical and duplicate IDs and records its rationale and source URLs. Matching ISO alone identifies the language context, not a dialect. Shared map coordinates often come from a parent or country approximation. Generic directional labels, parent-child relationships, competing aliases, incompatible classifications and country disagreements require separate consideration.

- **Kucha / Kucha: reject this proposed link.** `rolv:20345` is under Dawro (`dwr`), Ethiopia, in the Ta-Ne-Omotic family. `glottolog:kuch1247` is under Uyghur (`uig`), in Turkic. This is a same-name collision across unrelated classifications. [GRN Ethiopia](https://globalrecordings.net/en/country/ET), [Glottolog Kucha](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/kuch1247).
- **Luunda / Luunda: retain for classification review.** `rolv:00361` is under `rnd`; `glottolog:luun1238` is under `bem`. An exact name and overlapping country cannot resolve conflicting language assignments.

The same ancestor ISO does not justify flattening this hierarchy. [Glottolog reference](https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/41587).
- **Mwini: merge the corresponding Mwini pair, retain geographic review.** `rolv:16982` and `glottolog:mwin1241` share Af-Chimwiini, Barwaani, Bravanese, Chimiini, Chimwiini and Mwiini aliases under Swahili. Preserve both country claims and all source positions, and flag the surviving Mwini record for geographic review. GRN also lists a separate Kenya/Lamu Baravenes record; retain that record pending its own scope review. [GRN Mwini](https://globalrecordings.net/en/language/16982), [Glottolog Mwini](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/mwin1241).

## Global cohort adjudication

Three Luna agents at extra-high reasoning audited source correspondences, other
identifier families, and the reconciliation engine. The Astra lead rejected
independent-crosswalk wording for workbook-generated name joins, tightened inverse
alias uniqueness and direct country overlap, reviewed difficult cases and sampled
30 cohort pairs. A separate Luna critique checked all 2,509 selected pairs and
20 stratified examples, finding no concrete false positive. This is a reviewed
rule-based cohort inference, not an individual external attestation of every pair.

The dated source audit contains 8,062 candidate links. Its strict contextual
subset contains 2,923 pairs. Final selection requires matching immediate parent
and kind, exact non-generic current leaf, stable current/saved names, known parent
prefix where present, unique aliases in both directions, overlapping current
record countries, no children, no existing review flag, and at least two selected
siblings under the parent. Recounting siblings after the direct-country filter
leaves 2,509 pairs across 716 parent ISO cohorts and 95 countries. Two of these are
already in the four-variety Jumli cohort; adding the other two Jumli spelling
correspondences and the individually reviewed Mwini exception yields 2,512 merges.

All decisions name explicit IDs in
`data/language-atlas/reconciliation-decisions.json`. The 2,507 newly added cohort
decisions are labeled `reviewed-contextual-inference`; source evidence remains
attached to the canonical record. No fuzzy or generic-label matches are applied.
Short names (77 selected leaves of three or fewer letters) have the same strict
context and uniqueness checks but retain the uncertainty inherent in inference.

The remaining **5,550 candidate links are retained for review**, not established
duplicates. They include false-positive name collisions, hierarchy differences,
geographic disagreement, competing matches and insufficiently supported single
pairs. Candidate generation is not proof of identity. Other-source audit rows
include already-reconciled links and historical identifiers and must not be added
to these counts as distinct duplicate records.
