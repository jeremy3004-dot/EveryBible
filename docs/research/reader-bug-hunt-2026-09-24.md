## Checked and clean

- **The 2026-09-24 extraction refactors (68d8d89f, a0f7735a).** I compared them line by line with the inline code they replaced. `loadReaderChapter` keeps both staleness guards, the no-skeleton rule, the audio-only branch, `markChapterRead` only outside plans, and the check for a missing pack by name. `navigateListenChapter` reads the pause state when the button is pressed, as before. I found no regression.
- **Book and chapter boundaries.** Previous from Genesis 1 and next from Revelation 22 return `null`, and the reader disables the chevrons and swipes. Single-chapter books step to the neighbouring book. The next-chapter prefetch handles Jude to Revelation 1 and stops after Revelation 22. Psalm 119:176 parses; Psalm 119:200 and Psalm 151 are rejected.
- **Share and copy.** A selection belongs to one chapter and is cleared on any change of chapter, translation or focus verse, so a range cannot cross a chapter. Separate runs format as `1-3, 5`.
- **SQL wildcards and quotes in search.** `%`, `_`, `'` and `"` never reach SQL as operators, because the query is bound as a parameter and the words are FTS phrases. Uppercase `AND`, `OR` and `NOT` are quoted. Queries shorter than two characters, or with no letters or digits, never reach SQLite.
- **Search staleness.** The request id and cancel flag drop out-of-order results, and a translation change reruns the search.
- **Annotations after a translation switch.** Annotations are stored by book, chapter and verse without a translation, so they survive switching, hiding or deleting a translation. There is no dangling foreign key. `buildReaderHighlightIndex` clamps to the chapter's last verse.
- **Chapter selector.** The continue chapter is clamped to the book's range, and completed chapters outside the book are filtered out.
- **Reader with no text for a chapter.** It shows the "no verses" card instead of crashing. Audio-first translations with an EL chapter map skip chapters they do not cover.

## Known limitations (not fixed)

- **Versification.** Annotations and positions use the English (KJV/BSB) numbering. A translation with Hebrew Psalm numbering or a different Malachi 3/4 split would show highlights one verse off. Fixing this needs a versification map per translation.
- **Chinese, Japanese and Korean search.** FTS5 `unicode61` does not segment text without spaces, so a word inside a run of CJK characters is not findable, and the two-character minimum blocks one-character queries. Fixing this needs a trigram or ICU tokenizer, which means rebuilding the database. That was out of scope. **Fixed later on 2026-09-24 without a rebuild:** queries containing Han, kana, Hangul, Thai, Lao, Khmer or Myanmar characters skip FTS and run an `instr()` substring match scoped to the translation, in canonical order, capped at the limit. A single CJK character is a valid query. This also works on downloaded text packs, which ship without `verses_fts`. Latin-script searches on those packs still report "search unavailable". **Also fixed later:** the device now builds the pack's FTS index in the background and answers by substring until it is ready (see `pack-search-index-2026-09-24.md`).
- **Chapter arrows with partial audio coverage.** If a translation ever has both text and an EL audio chapter map, the read-mode chevrons would skip text chapters that have no audio. Today all EL translations are audio-only (`hasText: false`), so this is latent.
- **Note editor contents after the selection changes.** `AnnotationActionSheet` fills the note editor once, when it first opens. If the selection then grows onto a verse with a note, the editor stays empty. Low impact.
