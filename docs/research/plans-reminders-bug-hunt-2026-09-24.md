# Reading plans, streaks and reminders: bug hunt (2026-09-24)

Scope: reading-plan catalog and day logic, streaks and the reading calendar, and
local daily reminders. The plan sync/push path in `readingPlanService` was left
alone because a sibling agent was changing it (merge RPC).

All fixes were written test-first and are on this worktree branch, one commit
per fix.

## Checked, no bug found

- **Plan catalog integrity:** covered by `readingPlans.audit.test.ts`. Checks every advertised day, no gaps, unique entry ids, every passage resolving in the shipped BSB, whole-book coverage exactly once, localized titles in all locales, and recurring plans across leap years. All 23 plans were reviewed. The only multi-session plan is `kathisma-weekly`.
- **Day keys for streaks, plans and listening minutes:** all use the local calendar (`getFullYear/getMonth/getDate`). DST is covered by the existing New York tests, and early-morning reads in UTC+5:45 by the new Kathmandu tests. Leap days are handled by `setDate`.
- **Enrol, leave and re-join:** tombstones plus the `isEnrolmentEndedBy` check. There is no restart action; a completed plan can only be restarted by leaving and joining again.
- **Reboot and app update on Android:** expo-notifications 0.32 re-arms alarms on `BOOT_COMPLETED` and `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED`. It has no `TIMEZONE_CHANGED` receiver, which is why the reconciler is needed.
- **Duplicate reminders:** prevented by the stable identifier. The reconciler also runs its steps in sequence, so a cancel can't land after the schedule that followed it.
- **Reminder permission:** a denied or blocked permission is handled in Settings, including the route to system settings.

## Open (not fixed; needs a decision or out of scope)

1. **Tapping a notification goes nowhere** (low). The listener in `App.tsx` only logs. The daily reminder isn't tied to a plan, so there is no "right plan/day" to open. Opening Plans or Home would need a `data.screen` payload plus routing. That is a product decision.
2. **Enabled in the app but blocked by the OS** (low). If notification permission is revoked in system settings, the switch stays on and nothing tells the user.
3. **Latent: Monday-start weekly plans** (no current plan uses one). `getPlanCompletionEntryKey` and `getActivePlanDayNumber` assume the week starts on Sunday, while `getVisibleCompletedEntryCount` respects `weekStartsOn`. On a Sunday, Monday-start keys would point to next week.
4. **Latent: multi-session sequential plans** (no current plan is one). `markSessionComplete` sets `current_day = dayNumber` for a non-final session, which moves the pointer backwards when catching up an old day. It also sets `is_completed: false`, which would un-complete a finished plan.
5. **Cosmetic: monthly heatmap.** `ProgressCard` always draws 31 dots and says "of 31 days", while the ledger rows leave out days that don't exist this month (day 31 in September, days 29–31 in February).
6. **Home "Next up" ordering.** Home orders plans by `current_day`. For recurring plans that value is just the last day ticked, so a Proverbs plan can rank above Bible-in-a-Year for no real reason.
7. **Plan detail's `today` only refreshes on focus.** A screen left open across midnight shows yesterday until you navigate away and back. The 04:00 grace in fix 5 covers the completion side.
