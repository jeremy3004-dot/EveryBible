## Not fixed (needs a decision or out of scope)

- **Med (latent):** The Four Fields course content (section text, takeaways, discussion questions, practice activities, key-verse references) is English only and has no translation keys. It is rendered only by GroupDetail and GroupSession, and no screen navigates to `GroupList`, so users cannot reach it today. GroupSession also builds colours by concatenating hex strings (`colors.warning + '15'`).
- **Info:** `FELLOWSHIP_QUESTIONS` and `APPLICATION_QUESTIONS` in `gatherFoundations.ts` are English copies used only by tests. The screen uses the `gather.*Q*` keys.
- **Info:** No deep links go into lessons. `linkingConfig` covers only `bible/…` and `reset-password`. An unknown `lessonId`/`foundationId` shows a not-found state with a working Back button.

## Checked and fine

- **Progress scoping:** Both `gather-storage` (lesson marks) and `four-fields-storage` are in `PRIVATE_DATA_STORE_NAMES` and are registered with `registerPrivateDataStore`. Marks are stored per account, merged from guest on first sign-in, and survive restarts through MMKV. `privateDataAccounts.test.ts` covers switching accounts.
- **Sync:** Gather marks and Four Fields progress are local only and never synced. That matches the privateDataScope decision, but the CLAUDE.md line "Progress Tracking … syncs to Supabase" is out of date for course progress.
- **Mark/unmark:** Toggling works from both the lesson capsule and the bottom sheet, and GatherScreen subscribes to `completedLessons`, so counts update everywhere.
- **Non-English users:** Every foundation, wisdom and lesson title and every question comes from `gather.*` keys present in all 21 locales, which the coverage tests enforce. Book names are translated. The Bible text follows the reading translation, with the labelled BSB fallback above.
- **Colours:** No hard-coded colours in the Gather screens or components.
