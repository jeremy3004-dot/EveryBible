## Method

- Six native-quality reviewers worked in parallel, one per language group: hi/mr/ne, ar/ur/tr, pa/bn, ta/te,
  id/vi/zh/ja/ko and de/es/fr/pt/ru.
- Each reviewer had the English text, the key's UI context (from its call site in `src/`), and the plan's readings
  (`src/data/churchYearPlans.ts`, `easterCyclePlans.ts`, `autumnSeasonPlans.ts`). Each also had the locale's own
  established terms: book names, the Advent and Christmas plans, and the file's words for verse, download and
  offline.
- Descriptions were checked clause by clause against the English for dropped content.
- I cross-checked every proposed change myself, made three calls across locales (marked "Coordinator" in the
  tables), and applied the fixes by key path with a TypeScript-AST script.
- A guard script compared every locale against the sprint branch tip. Only in-scope keys changed (plus the one
  indirect key below), every `{{token}}` still matches English, the keyset and key order are unchanged, and there
  is no mojibake (U+0080–U+009F, U+FFFD) anywhere in the locale files.
- I rendered `seasonStarts` with real localized dates (for example `Başlangıç: 10 Şub Çar`, `2月10日(水)開始`,
  `बुध, 10 फ़र॰`) to confirm the sentence works for every date.

### Findings by defect class

- **Religious register.**
  - pa: the risen Lord "ਦਰਸ਼ਨ ਦਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ" used the Sikh/Hindu idiom for a guru or deity granting darshan. It now
    says ਵਿਖਾਈ ਦਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ, as in the Punjabi Bible (Luke 24:34). The pa Last Supper ਅੰਤਿਮ ਭੋਜਨ went with funeral
    rites (ਅੰਤਿਮ ਅਰਦਾਸ) and is now the file's ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਭੋਜਨ.
  - tr: Jerusalem was Kudüs, the secular and Islamic name, in three descriptions. It is now Yeruşalim, as in the
    Kutsal Kitap and the file's Acts plan.
  - hi: the Epiphany title "light to the जातियों" read first as "castes". It now uses the Bible's अन्यजातियों
    (Isa 49:6). Tribe is कुल.
  - ur: the Orthodox calendar was جنتری, an almanac booklet with astrology connotations.
  - ja and ko were brought into line with each file's Protestant usage:
    - ja: 受難節, 棕櫚の主日 and 全聖徒の日. The Epiphany subtitle 諸国民の光 was the Japanese title of a Vatican
      II document.
    - ko: 만성절 instead of the Catholic 모든 성인의 날.
  - mr: the cross is वधस्तंभ, as in the Marathi Bible and the rest of the file, not the Catholic क्रूस.
  - zh: 帕斯卡 is how Chinese writes the mathematician Pascal. The Orthodox plan now says 复活节（巴斯哈）.
  - No Islamic substitutions (عیسیٰ, نماز, Allah in Turkish, رزق), Sikh liturgy words, or Hindu-mythology words
    appear in the reviewed strings.
- **Inverted or shifted meaning.**
  - ko: Lent ended at 나사로의 살아남, Lazarus _surviving_. It now says Jesus raised him.
  - te: Lazarus was "getting up", as from sleep. He is now raised from the dead.
  - Pentecost asked readers to pray _on behalf of_ (or _to_) the Holy Spirit in ar, es, fr, pt, ru, id and vi.
    It now prays for the Spirit's coming.
  - ur: the Easter plan ran to the evening _of_ Ascension Day, not its eve.
  - id: "Minggu kedua November" means the second _week_. It is now the second Sunday.
  - "In season" read as produce, fashion, weather or slang in 10 locales and was replaced in each: de, es, fr, pt,
    ru, tr, pa, id, vi and zh. The ar, ur, ja and ko wording was checked and kept; see their tables.
  - bn ক্ষতি and pa ਘਾਟਾ for "loss" read as harm and deficit. They are now শোক and ਸੋਗ, the words each file
    uses in its Loss plan.
- **Calques and literal renderings that read wrongly.**
  - The Orthodox "Mystical Supper" became "a mysterious (or mystic) feast/dinner" in hi, mr, ne, bn, pa, ur, tr,
    ko and zh.
    - Where the language has an established Orthodox term, it is used: ar العشاء السري, ja 機密の晩餐,
      ko 신비의 만찬, zh 奥秘晚餐 (奥秘 is the Chinese Bible's word for mystery), and the European languages'
      Mystical Supper.
    - Elsewhere the plan says Last Supper in the file's own term.
  - Hebrews 12:1 "great cloud of witnesses" is fixed in ten locales. It now follows each language's Bible:
    - It was a word-for-word calque in ja, ta and id.
    - ur made the cloud itself run the race. pa doubled a word (ਦੌੜ ਦੌੜ). pt said "correram a corrida".
    - Russian sports jargon (дистанция) became the Synodal поприще. tr uses the Bible's tanıklar.
    - The "cloud" image was restored in te and ko.
  - Hard Christmas calqued "heavy with" in de, es, fr, pt, ru, pa and zh. zh was also ungrammatical (被失去).
- **Silently dropped content.** zh, ja and vi had dropped "beginnings" from the New Year plan, and zh had dropped
  "Holy Week" from the Orthodox plan.
- **Form of address.** fr switched to "tu" in three descriptions; the file uses "vous" everywhere. ar used the
  plural command where the file speaks to the reader in the singular.
- **Accessibility.** The background-tile label read "3/12" (ja, ko, tr), which a screen reader can read as a date
  or a fraction. It now follows each file's "X of Y" pattern.
- **Consistency with the file.**
  - Book names now match `bible.books.*`: ta and te Acts, ne Daniel.
  - Jerusalem is spelled as in each Bible: bn যিরূশালেম, tr Yeruşalim.
  - Each file's own terms are now used: te పంచుకోండి for Share, vi Ảnh nền for background, bn পটভূమি,
    hi शिष्य, mr सुवार्ता, ko 번역자, and te/ta "God's Word" where a bare "word" read as "a word" or "a sentence".
  - Pentecost is spelled as each Bible spells it: ne पेन्तिकोस, ur پنتکست.
- **Grammar.** hi and mr offline notices had number-agreement errors. ru Easter had an ungrammatical double
  participle. Other fixes: tr Paskalya Pazartesisi, the ne "Starts" participle, a doubled "até" in pt, and
  capitalised Last Supper / Lunes de Pascua in de/es.
- **No problems found:**
  - `{{interpolation}}` tokens and plural forms (none of these keys is a count key).
  - Mojibake and leftover English.
  - `sessionWindow` in every locale.
  - `seasonStarts`: no locale glues a case ending to `{{date}}`, and every construction works for any date.

### Needs a native speaker

- **te `readingPlans.churchYear.lent.title`** (ఉపవాస కాలం, "fasting season"). It is correct and safe. Many Telugu
  Protestant churches say శ్రమ కాలం instead, and a Telugu church member should choose which one this app's
  readers know. Do not use తపస్సు కాలం: తపస్సు is Hindu ascetic language.

These lower-confidence calls were settled but would benefit from a native check:

- bn Palm Sunday খেজুর পাতার রবিবার (the old খেজুর রবিবার reads as "date-fruit Sunday").
- tr oymak for "tribe": the reviewer believes Rev 5:9 and 7:9 use it in the Kutsal Kitap.
- ur Lent stays لینٹ کا موسم; Catholics also say چالیسہ.
- hi Lent stays चालीसा काल, an established Christian Hindi term.
- mr and ne gloss the Orthodox पास्का as "that is, Easter", because both files use other words for Passover.

- **`readingPlans.dayOf`** and **`readingPlans.churchYear.heading`** have a new place: the Home season card and the
  seasonal plans' category label. Both read correctly there in all 20 locales, so the verdict is ok × 20 each.
- **`audio.nextChapter`** and **`bible.nextChapterHint`**: the sprint tip (`5c81d9b7`) made `audio.nextChapter`
  the reader bar's next-button label, paired with `audio.previousChapter`, and moved `bible.nextChapterHint` to
  its screen-reader hint.
  - The labels pair correctly in all 20 locales, so the verdict is ok × 20.
  - As a hint, 19 locales read descriptively, so they are ok. The exception was Indonesian: "Buka pasal
    berikutnya" was the imperative written for its old use as the label. Indonesian hints in this file are
    descriptive (Membuka…, Menandai…), so it now reads **"Membuka pasal berikutnya"**. Committed as `25dd82b7`.
- `common.error` and `common.unexpectedError` are reused for new failure alerts (language switch, reminder cancel,
  chapter feedback). Their meaning is unchanged, so they are ok.
