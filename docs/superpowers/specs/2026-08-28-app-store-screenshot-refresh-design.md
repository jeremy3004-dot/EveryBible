# Every Bible App Store Screenshot Refresh

## Goal

Replace the red iPhone marketing screenshots with a four-slide 6.9-inch App Store set inspired by the Bible App's warm editorial presentation. The new set should feel calm, modern, Scripture-centered, and unmistakably aligned with Every Bible's blue-and-ivory identity.

## Deliverables

- Four portrait PNGs at 1320 × 2868 pixels.
- A contact sheet for review.
- A manifest recording source screenshots, slide copy, dimensions, and production method.
- A versioned output folder at `store-metadata/screenshots/ios/iphone-69-2026-08-28/`.

## Slide Story

1. Home: **Start each day in Scripture**
2. Bible reader: **Read without distraction**
3. Gather foundations: **Grow as a disciple**
4. Reading plans: **Build a daily rhythm**

Each headline communicates one benefit and remains readable at App Store thumbnail size.

## Art Direction

- Warm ivory and parchment backgrounds with restrained Every Bible blue accents.
- One darker or cooler contrast treatment may be used for rhythm while remaining part of the same system.
- Bold editorial headline above the device.
- The supplied app screen remains the visual center of every slide.
- Subtle paper grain, soft light, and simple hand-drawn or geometric accents may support the composition without competing with the UI.
- No red backgrounds, red app tint, generic religious stock imagery, or visual clutter.

## Product-Screen Invariants

- Use the four supplied 1320 × 2868 screenshots exactly as captured.
- Do not regenerate, retouch, recolor, rewrite, crop into, or obscure the app UI.
- Place each screen inside a clean dark iPhone frame with natural corner radii and a restrained shadow.
- Preserve legibility of the status bar, navigation, Scripture, and feature content.

## Production Method

1. Generate the editorial background artwork with the built-in Image 2.0 workflow.
2. Normalize each background to the 1320 × 2868 App Store canvas.
3. Add approved typography and place the exact source screenshot deterministically inside the centered device frame.
4. Export flattened RGB PNGs with zero-padded filenames.

## Quality Gate

- Exactly 1320 × 2868 pixels per slide.
- RGB PNG without alpha.
- No clipped headline, device, or app content.
- No altered or AI-redrawn UI.
- Consistent palette and typography across all four slides.
- Headline readable within one second at reduced preview size.
- Filenames sort in intended upload order.
